## About

You know the feeling: an idea arrives at 2am with a rush of energy — you can
see the life it belongs to. Then sleep, work, and everything else bury it.
Next time it surfaces, weeks are gone. Sometimes it never surfaces at all.

**Midnight Momentum catches those sparks before they fade — and brings them
back to you.** A journal, an AI companion, and a night sky that slowly fills
with everything you've kept.

No streaks that shame you. No goals someone else handed you. Only yours.

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Moments** | Your journal as a constellation — every thought, plan, and spark strung on a thread through time |
| **Capture anywhere** | Type it, speak it, share it straight from Instagram/TikTok/YouTube, answer from the notification shade — even fully offline |
| **Eva** | An AI companion with one continuous memory across text, voice, and your journal — she remembers what you were becoming · [view repository →](https://github.com/Omar11N/EVA-Chatbot_MidnightMomentum) |
| **Aspirations** | Living things, not checkboxes — they burn, cool, and rest, never with guilt. Realized ones become stars in your sky |
| **A spark, returned** | Each day the app brings back one moment from your past — chosen by what you're living through right now |
| **Letters from Eva** | A weekly letter, written from everything she's seen you carry |
| **Eight Midnights** | Themes as dedications — every palette is a person, every sky a different night |

## Tech Stack

**Mobile**
- React Native (Expo) — iOS & Android
- GPU-rendered night sky (Skia shaders)

**Backend**
- Python Flask · PostgreSQL · deployed on Railway
- AI: Google Gemini — semantic memory & retrieval built in-house

**Website**
- Node.js + Express · deployed on Railway

## Philosophy

Built by one founder, at midnight, since August 2025 — for the people whose
ideas deserve better than a notes-app graveyard. Intrinsic motivation only:
the app never tells you what you *should* want. It keeps what you already
wanted, alive.
